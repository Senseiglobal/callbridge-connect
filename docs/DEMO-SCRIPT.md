# Three-minute video plan

The final [2:57 submission video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FKvPDZw4aMw) is already recorded and uploaded. It contains an AI-narrated introduction, the actual Aura form and saved request, the genuine two-way call, and the completed result. See [the verification record](VERIFIED-AURA-CALLBACK.md). No additional call is needed. The outline below is the earlier planning guide, not a transcript or exact timing of the final edit.

Use English. Record genuine behavior. Do not imply the fictional preview is a phone conversation. Keep keys, access codes and full phone numbers off screen. Obtain the speaker's permission to record and publish their voice.
